# FAQ and troubleshooting

## Frequently asked questions

<details>

<summary>Is ProductReels really free?</summary>

Yes. Every available feature is free, with no Pro version and no license key.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need WooCommerce?</summary>

No. Video reels, galleries, custom buttons and analytics work on any WordPress site. Product tagging, product cards and add to cart need WooCommerce.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I add reels to WooCommerce product pages?</summary>

Use automatic product-page placement, a shortcode, the Gutenberg block or the Elementor widget. See [WooCommerce product pages](product-pages.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I put a reels slider on my homepage?</summary>

Yes. Choose a carousel, grid, marquee or another [layout](layouts.md) and place the widget on your homepage or any landing page.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do TikTok or Instagram reels import automatically?</summary>

No. Upload videos you own as MP4 files, or use YouTube Shorts, Vimeo, or hosted MP4/WebM links.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I track sales generated by my videos?</summary>

Yes. [Statistics](statistics.md) show views, clicks, click rate, and eligible paid orders and revenue linked to add-to-cart actions from reels. Cancelled, refunded and failed orders are excluded.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does it work with Elementor and Gutenberg?</summary>

Yes, both are supported, along with shortcodes. See [Placing widgets](placing-widgets.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is the player mobile-friendly?</summary>

Yes. Layouts are responsive and the fullscreen player supports swipe navigation and accessible controls. See [The shopper experience](shopper-experience.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens to my data if I uninstall?</summary>

Your data is kept by default. Turn on **Delete all data on uninstall** in [Settings](settings.md#data) to remove all plugin tables and options when the plugin is deleted.

</details>

## Troubleshooting

### My widget does not appear

1. Check the shortcode's ID matches your widget (copy it from the **Widgets** screen).
2. Make sure the widget has at least one reel in its **Reels** step.
3. Log in as an administrator and look at the page: a short notice such as *"widget #12 was not found"* means the ID is wrong. Visitors never see this notice.
4. Save the widget once. This clears its cache.

### My changes do not show on the live site

ProductReels clears a widget's cache whenever you save it. If changes still do not appear, clear any **page cache** from a caching plugin, CDN or host. See [Settings → Performance](settings.md#performance).

### A video will not play

* **MP4** plays everywhere, so use it for uploads.
* Browsers start videos **muted** until a visitor taps once. This is a browser rule.
* For YouTube Shorts or Vimeo, check the video is public and embeddable.

### I cannot tag products

Product tagging needs **WooCommerce** to be active. If it is off, the reel editor shows a notice with a link to your **Plugins** screen.

### Views or clicks look too low

* A view counts only after **one second of playback**.
* Each visitor can add a limited number of views per reel in a time window. See [Settings → View tracking](settings.md#view-tracking).

### Orders or revenue are missing

Only **paid** orders that contain a product **added to the cart from a reel** are counted. Cancelled, refunded and failed orders are excluded.

### The widget does not show on product pages

1. Open the widget's **Place it** step and confirm **Show on product pages** is on.
2. **Save the widget**: placement applies once it is saved.
3. If the page is empty for some products, the widget is set to **Current product's reels** and those products have no tagged reels. Add fallback reels in the **Reels** step. See [WooCommerce product pages](product-pages.md).
4. Try another **Position**: some themes place WooCommerce sections differently.

### The popup does not appear

* Check its **Trigger** (page load, delay or scroll) and that the visitor has scrolled far enough.
* A visitor who closed it earlier will not see it again until its **Show again** period ends. Test in a private browser window.
* Make sure **Show on mobile** is on if you are testing on a phone.
